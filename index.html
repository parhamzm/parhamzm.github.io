About

I am a machine learning researcher building practical AI at the intersection of deep learning, generative models and computer vision — and I care as much about whether a model can be trusted as about what it can produce.

I completed my M.Sc. in Computer Engineering at the University of Tehran as the top-ranked graduate of my cohort. My thesis, supervised by Dr. Mehdi Modarressi and Dr. Mohammad Amin Sadeghi, asked whether a generative model could recover what video compression throws away. The answer was NU-Class Net — a network that restores low-bitrate video well enough to cut file sizes roughly sevenfold without the viewer noticing, published in Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence.

That question — what can we take away, and what must we keep? — still drives most of my work. Most recently, as a researcher at the Qatar Computing Research Institute, I was a core contributor to Fanar, an Arabic-centric multimodal generative AI platform. I led culturally aligned image generation — teaching a model to render Arabic-Islamic visual culture accurately rather than approximately — and built the evaluation toolbox and benchmarking pipeline whose metrics were eventually adopted across the whole project.

At the School of ECE in Tehran I work across two labs. In the Computer Vision Lab I study reliability, robustness and fairness in generative and vision models. In the Intelligent Architectures for Computing Systems Lab I make deep models small and fast enough to run where they are actually needed — wearable health devices, edge hardware, and memory-bound systems — through pruning, quantization and hardware-aware design.

I like problems that refuse to stay theoretical. Alongside research I have shipped computer vision into production, founded a marketplace startup, taught ten university courses, and written and filmed explanations of the ideas I find beautiful. I was admitted twice to the Ph.D. program in Computer Science at the University of California, Irvine (Fall 2024 and Fall 2025) but could not enroll because of U.S. visa processing delays — so I am actively looking for a Ph.D. position where this work can continue.

“He who knows best, knows how little he knows.”
