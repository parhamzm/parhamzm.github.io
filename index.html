I am a researcher at the University of Tehran. Since May 2026 I have been working with Dr. Mehrdad Tajbakhsh on accountability for autonomous agents. We are developing ways for an agent to record, explain, and defend its actions, and studying sovereign AI: how institutional control over AI infrastructure affects whether agents can be governed. As part of the same project, I am building an open-weight language model that assists experts with intangible-asset valuation. It draws on domain knowledge through retrieval and is designed to protect the information users share with it.

Before that, I spent a year as a researcher at the Qatar Computing Research Institute, working on Fanar, an Arabic-centric multimodal generative AI platform. I led the benchmarking of its culturally aligned image generation. I built the evaluation suite, designed fifteen LLM-as-a-judge metrics for cultural accuracy and image quality that the team used in model selection, and measured how well the automated judges agreed with Elo rankings from human pairwise comparisons. A manuscript on this work, Fanar Oryx, is in preparation [6].

I earned my M.Sc. in Computer Engineering at the University of Tehran in 2023 and graduated first among the Computer Architecture students. My thesis, advised by Dr. Mehdi Modarressi and Dr. Mohammad Amin Sadeghi, introduced NU-Class Net, a network that restores the quality of low-bitrate compressed video after standard decoding, with no change to the codec. It was published in Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence in 2025 [1].

I still work with two labs in Tehran. In the Computer Vision Lab, with Dr. Mostafa Tavassolipour, I am developing a generative tool that proposes carpet patterns for designers to refine, with algorithms that keep every proposal within the palette and pixel-grid limits of machine weaving. In the Intelligent Architectures for Computing Systems Lab, with Dr. Modarressi, I work on compact deep learning models for EEG analysis on wearable devices [5] and coordinate the lab’s reading group on LLM hardware and efficiency.

I have also been a teaching assistant for eight university courses, five of them as head TA, including Trustworthy AI at the University of Tehran since 2023. I was admitted to the Ph.D. program in Computer Science at UC Irvine for Fall 2024 and again for Fall 2025, but could not enroll because of U.S. visa delays. I am now looking for a Ph.D. position where I can continue this research.

Research directions

  1. Evaluating AI systems and holding agents accountable

    Methods for judging generated content, including LLM judges compared against human preferences, and mechanisms that let autonomous agents record, explain, and defend their decisions.

    Related: [6] and my current project

  2. Generative models with cultural and physical constraints

    Image generation aligned with Arabic-Islamic visual culture, and carpet-pattern generation in which every design must fit a limited palette and the pixel grid of a weaving machine.

    Related: [6] and the Computer Vision Lab project

  3. Efficient deep learning

    Getting more out of limited computation: restoring compressed video without changing the codec, compact EEG models for wearable devices, and deep learning acceleration in 3D-stacked memory.

    Related: [1] [5] [2] [7]
